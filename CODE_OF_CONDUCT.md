# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Eqiora contributors and maintainers are committed to a respectful,
harassment-free, and technically rigorous community for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, nationality, appearance, disability, or
affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas with evidence and precision; do not attack people.
- State assumptions, uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and limitations.
- Welcome good-faith questions and make specialized knowledge accessible.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept correction, make amends when you cause harm, and put community safety first.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, and retaliation are not tolerated in project spaces or when
representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the bootstrap maintainer using the contact
method on the GitHub profile listed in `CODEOWNERS`. Do not open a public issue
containing sensitive personal information. Reports will be acknowledged,
investigated impartially, and shared only as needed for enforcement.

Possible responses include a private warning, content removal, temporary
restriction, removal from project roles, or a permanent ban. Maintainers with a
conflict of interest must recuse themselves. A public transparency summary may
be issued without identifying affected people.

This policy applies to repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions,
events, and other spaces officially associated with Eqiora.
