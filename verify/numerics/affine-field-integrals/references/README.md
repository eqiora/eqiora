# Mathematical reference

For an affine field its gradient is constant, its mean on a segment is the mean
of its endpoint values, and its mean on a triangle is the mean of its vertex
values. Flux through a straight edge is the dot product with its outward unit
normal times edge length. The [case derivation](../README.md) applies these
identities directly to the declared vertices and conductivity, independently
of finite-element assembly and observation output.
