# Model-driven common-Mesh admission

This case selects the crate-private mathematical admission owner used by the
root `Plan` path. A fresh external-support scalar Model is admitted as
Q1 and TPFA over the same caller-owned `2 x 3` Cartesian Mesh. The independent
analytic cardinalities are twelve vertex values for Q1 and six cell values for
TPFA; their effective policy identities must differ while the caller Model
digest remains exact.

Scalar conservation and the structurally different vector small-strain form
derive their spatial, formulation, algebraic-structure, temporal, scaling, and
linear-solver requirements before entering one shared linear resolution path.
A one-, two-, and three-Field coupled scalar composition uses the same owner
without adding a physics-family discriminator. Independent discrete and
elasticity equilibrium oracles close ordinary execution and replay.

Renaming a scalar Component, Field, and Relations changes exact Model and Plan
identity while preserving the admitted formulation, solver requirements, and
independent solution. A compiler-valid field-dependent constitutive-flux
mutation fails at the concrete scalar conservation-form check. Replacing one
prescribed trace by a prescribed outward flux fails the requested exact
complete-essential primal form at its boundary-law check.

The same private core also retains bounded transient incompressible Models over
caller-owned affine-triangle and Cartesian resources, resolving MINI/P1 and
cell-centered policies without changing Model meaning.

Causal negatives additionally replace scalar diffusion with an algebraic reaction, remove
incompressibility from an otherwise flow-shaped Model, cross-wire the scalar
Model with MINI/P1, independently substitute algorithm, preconditioner, and
reduction, reject a negative-zero tolerance, and attempt execution through a
foreign solver provider. Resource
falsifiers replace the exact Geometry, substitute a same-cardinality Cartesian
Mesh with different axes, swap left/right source-facet assignments, mutate
production provider and correspondence identity. Repeated identical admission
has one policy identity; changing method or provider changes it.

The case does not claim arbitrary equations or formulations, arbitrary geometry
or mesh, nonorthogonal TPFA, a general temporal method, or removal of the typed
Plan and execution payload sums.
