# Independent expectations

For N=4 the forward roots are `[1,i,-1,-i]`. At `t=1+n/2`, the exponential
`(2+3i)exp(-i*pi*(t-1))` has samples `[z,-iz,-z,iz]`. Multiplying each by the
roots for bin 1 makes all four terms z, so C1=z; other bins cancel. Frequency
is 1/(4*0.5)=0.5 Hz, angular frequency pi rad/s, and phase atan2(3,2).
Power is 13 m², density 26 m²s, rectangle estimate 4+6i m s.

The real sinusoid `4sin(pi*(t-1))` gives `[0,4,0,-4]`: C1=2i and C3=-2i.
Mean square `(0+16+0+16)/4=8` equals summed bin power 4+4; the one-sided
amplitude is 4 and one-sided density integrates over df=0.5 to power 8.
DC 2 plus Nyquist cosine amplitude 3 tests that neither special bin doubles.

For the off-bin carrier exp(-i*pi*n/4), put a=1/sqrt(2). Its samples are
`[1,a-ia,-i,-a-ia]`. Every coefficient has real part 1/4; imaginary parts are
`[-(1+2a),1+2a,2a-1,1-2a]/4`. Thus multiple bins carry positive energy.
Periodic Hann at N=4 is `[0,1/2,1,1/2]`, unlike the symmetric N-1 window.
Applied to constant 2, inverse values are `[0,1,2,1]`, DC is 1, and C1=-1/2.

Six channel amplitudes 1 through 6 multiply the same carrier independently.
Five constant finite-basis coordinates retain their original basis under complex
promotion. No result generated by the transform is used to set these expectations.
Absolute tolerance 1e-12 covers a small number of binary64 elementary operations;
it is not a continuous-time integration or frequency-resolution error bound.

For odd N=3, the samples [1,0,0] have Ck=1/3 in every bin; the highest
positive bin is still an interior bin and doubles in a one-sided amplitude.
The polynomial (2t-3)(t-2) provides those samples at t=1,1.5,2 without an
implementation-generated reference. An accepted initial State provides t=0
for a constant-signal test even though the output schedule starts at t=0.5.
