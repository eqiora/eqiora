| Uniform Relation equation authoring | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Relations take optional `on` support and `at` Clock clauses and keep `lhs = rhs` with source ranges; both sides are type-checked, and expression depth is limited to 256. | Finite affine equality, inequality, and complementarity | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Finite affine equalities, inequalities and complementarity conditions run through the no-Mesh Plan/Run/Result path with explicit tolerances and an active-set budget, and every original condition is rechecked after the solve and on replay. Not yet: contact search, friction, nonlinear constraints, penalty or augmented methods, nonsmooth derivatives. |
| Strict-interior finite nonlinear equality | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Finite nonlinear equalities with strict inequality margins solve by Newton with explicit seeds and linear controls, real or complex, with regularity checked at the accepted point and replay rechecking the equations; [Constraints guide](../language/constraints.md#strict-interior-nonlinear-execution). Not yet: branch tracking, reduced-solution derivatives, complementarity, spatial nonlinear constraints. |
| Typed source-to-transaction lowering | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Scalar models, local and package hierarchies, a packaged 2D boundary interface, and closed 2D elasticity and steady Newtonian fluid Models lower through the ordinary Transaction path, with packages contributing typed slots and tensor Relations. Not yet: general arrays, tensor algebra, constitutive libraries, a general package executor; [`language.canonical-tensor-operators`](../../verify/language/canonical-tensor-operators/README.md), [`solid.packaged-isotropic-balance-2d`](../../verify/solid/packaged-isotropic-balance-2d/README.md), [`fluid.packaged-steady-stokes-2d`](../../verify/fluid/packaged-steady-stokes-2d/README.md) |
| Shared Rust `Module` AST | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | The Rust `Module` AST builds typed Fields, Parameters, Relations, Domains, Ports and Connections through the same lowering as source, including Cartesian boxes with `grad`, `div` and `trace`; registered execution is 1D FEM. Not yet: broader spatial operators; [`interfaces.python-native-modeling`](../../verify/interfaces/python-native-modeling/README.md) |
| Explicit Cartesian coefficient values | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | `tensor_value(frame = support, components = ...)` (Python `Parameter(frame=...)`, `lang.tensor_value`) builds real or complex vector and tensor coefficients on a Cartesian support, keeping component order and units through edits and replay. Covered by product tests. Not yet: tensor contraction, local frames, complex numerical execution. |
| Offline agent-proposed model change | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | An offline proposal picks a scalar Parameter and value; Eqiora builds, previews and commits the child Model and replays it, and a 1D FEM check confirms the result against registered evidence. Stale, foreign, no-op or inconsistent proposals are rejected. No agent schema, synthesis or approval workflow; [`interfaces.agent-authored-model-change`](../../verify/interfaces/agent-authored-model-change/README.md) |
| Static name resolution | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | Source imports use complete canonical module identities and are the only way to introduce a local namespace alias. Local modules and direct dependencies resolve; private, unknown, transitive, wildcard, and selective access fail closed. Registered verification covers the earlier exact-package subset. |
| Static unit checking | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | Scalar paths and the bounded tensor-structure relation preserve coherent SI dimensions. Hierarchical source checking and committed semantic validation share the identity-parametric tensor rules; flat lowering preserves an uncommitted canonical Transaction and whole-model admission remains authoritative. General tensor algebra and library-scale diagnostics remain |
| Coherent derived-dimension aliases | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | The dimension names `Hz`, `N`, `Pa`, `J` and `W` are built in beside the seven SI base symbols and expand exactly to base exponents. Covered by product tests. Not yet: prefixes, scaled or affine units, display units, nondimensionalization. |
| Compilation-unit structural dimension aliases | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | `dimension Name = expression;` (private or `public`) defines structural unit aliases across a module and its direct imports; Python `Module.dimension` and `ModuleRef.dimension` use the same resolver, and cycles, duplicates and unknown names are rejected. Not yet: prefixes, scales, offsets, quantity kinds. |
| Real/complex typed arithmetic and finite execution | ✅ | 🟨 | ⬜ | ⬜ | Expressions evaluate real and complex binary64 values with arithmetic, powers, channel arithmetic, indexing and `conj`, `real`, `imag`, `abs`, `abs2`, `arg`, `sin`, `cos`, `exp`, `log`, `sqrt`; finite affine complex Models run through Plan, Run and Result with the selected provider's complex solver and no fallback. Not yet: complex ordering, complex division and powers in operator lowering, automatic complex solver ranking. |
| Scalar arithmetic expression language | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | SSA lowering exists; full numerical function catalog remains |
| Canonical scalar-mathematics namespace and typed derived aliases | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | The `math` namespace gives `pi`, `i`, `complex`, `sin`, `cos`, `exp`, `log`, `sqrt`, `conj`, `real`, `imag`, `abs`, `abs2`, `arg`. `let` aliases in Models and Components (Python `Component.let_alias`) name static or runtime expressions with inferred or annotated types, optional `on` and `at` checks, and correct derivatives. Not yet: context-dependent spatial aliases, more math functions. |
| Spatial differential expressions | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | `grad`, `div`, `trace`, `normal`, and coordinates for bounded scalar paths. Shared element-wise type rules additionally retain nested channel axes through gradients, divergence and normal contraction without treating channel axes as spatial axes. Focused tests cover real/complex domains, dimensions, parent support and equal-sized channel/spatial distinction; ordinary multicomponent spatial assembly and execution remain in progress. |
| Canonical tensor structure expressions | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | `symmetric_part` turns a Cartesian `[d,d]` tensor into its symmetric part and `isotropic_lift` turns a scalar into `s I_d`, with dimensions from the support; both survive lowering and replay. Not tensor algebra, an elasticity package or a solve by itself; [`language.canonical-tensor-operators`](../../verify/language/canonical-tensor-operators/README.md), [RFC 0038](../../rfcs/0038-canonical-tensor-structure-operators.md) |
| Time-state expressions | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | Current, derivative, pre, and next forms exist. Continuous derivatives require continuous states; clocked pre/next references retain exact Clock identity. Continuous event reset next-state remains admitted. A state declaration does not prove independent state count or well-posedness. |
| Clocked algebraic Variables | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | A real scalar Variable can have one Clock and is solved at its ticks, with no value between them and no memory; nonunique seeds are rejected without committing the tick. Not yet: shaped or distributed clocked algebraic Variables. |
| Exact integer values | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Signed 64-bit integers stay exact through literals, Parameters, expressions and replay, with checked overflow and explicit real conversion; sampled discrete assignments use them. | Boolean values and exact scalar predicates | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Boolean Parameters, inputs, outputs and State work in source, native, Python and sampled execution, with exact comparisons and short-circuit `and`/`or`; Boolean arithmetic and implicit Boolean solves are rejected. Covered by product tests. |
| Atomic nominal spaces and counts | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | `FiniteSpace` defines an ordered nominal basis; signed coordinates and counts keep it, and equal labels do not make two spaces equal. Not yet: product counts, basis transforms, chemical conservation. |
| Finite real/complex component algebra | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Finite spaces support `linear_map`, `apply`, `compose`, `pair`, `transpose`, `adjoint`, `tensor_product` and `permute_factors` with typed units, and finite affine Models using them run through Plan/Run/Result. | Finite-map invariants and real inverse actions | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Finite maps support trace and identity (real or complex) and real `determinant` and `inverse` with row-pivoted LU and wide-range exponents, with forward, reverse and mixed derivatives and unit-aware results; ill-conditioned inverses are rejected rather than regularized. | Nominal index sets and static instance families | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | `IndexSet` gives indexes a nominal type; source instance families, indexed Relations and connections expand with exact member identity, and finite `sum`/`product`/`min`/`max` work over index sets. | Conditional scalar values and point-bound derivatives | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | [Conditional scalar values](../language/conditionals.md) type-check both branches and evaluate only the selected one; operators such as abs, min, max, clamp, sign and step share the kernel definitions, and linearization rejects points on a switching boundary. | Source crossing events and reset aliases | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | [Crossing events](../language/events.md) take one scalar guard with an explicit direction and share one activation across their reset Relations; source bouncing-ball and thermostat fixtures match reference and CPU execution.
