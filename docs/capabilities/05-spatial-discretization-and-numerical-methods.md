# Spatial discretization and numerical methods

[Capability matrix](../capability-matrix.md) · [legend](../capability-matrix.md#how-to-read-the-matrix)

| Capability | C | X | V | M | Current implementation and planned extensions |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|---|
| Continuous Galerkin FEM | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | Scalar P1/Q1 elliptic paths, 2D Cartesian Q1 isotropic elasticity, the nondimensional affine-triangle MINI convergence slice, and one exact canonical/package coherent-SI Field-wise MINI bridge |
| Fixed-domain harmonic response restriction | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | Explicit Formulations turn real linear time-invariant models into complex amplitude problems at a given frequency, then reconstruct real signals; the registered case checks RC/RLC circuits, power and a small damped wave. Not yet: general Fourier transforms, nonlinear harmonic balance, mixed DC, response derivatives, frequency sweeps; [`numerics.harmonic-response`](../../verify/numerics/harmonic-response/README.md). |
| Authored scalar weak form | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | A Component can author a named scalar weak form with explicit test function and zero-trace boundaries, in source or Python; Cartesian Q1 checks it against the strong form and runs steady heat and mass problems, including nonzero boundary values and constant-capacity backward Euler. Not yet: nonlinear storage, multidimensional integral forms, general transient weak forms, registered verification. |
| Explicit mixed residual rows | ✅ | 🟨 | ⬜ | ⬜ | Each authored equation names its test Field, so mixed multi-field problems do not depend on declaration order; source `derive ... using test` and Python `tests=` produce the same equations. Tests check two- and three-field linear and nonlinear steps against an independent reference. Not yet: spatial convergence, general Green identities, stability, registered verification. |
| Authored steady Stokes mixed inspection | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | A 2D steady Stokes weak form can be authored and inspected; its viscous, pressure, forcing and continuity terms are checked against the live Stokes model before Python shows or renders it. Not yet: running authored mixed forms, nonzero velocity boundaries, transient, complex or 3D forms, inf-sup analysis, registered verification. |
| Authored scalar interval conservation | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | A steady 1D scalar conservation law can be authored over an arbitrary interval (endpoint fluxes plus source) and is checked against the Geometry; the 1D TPFA Plan runs authored and automatic forms and keeps their provenance. Not yet: storage, multidimensional or mixed forms, registered verification. |
| Derived FEM local forms | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | A private, certified derivation compiles Poisson to Q1 element kernels with JVP/VJP in 1D–3D, and 2D Q1 elasticity to one kernel. Not yet: source weak forms, Robin boundaries, simplex or high-order spaces, JIT, device runs; [`numerics.compiled-cartesian-poisson-q1-2d`](../../verify/numerics/compiled-cartesian-poisson-q1-2d/README.md), [`numerics.compiled-cartesian-elasticity-q1-2d`](../../verify/numerics/compiled-cartesian-elasticity-q1-2d/README.md), [`numerics.cartesian-poisson-3d-fem-fvm`](../../verify/numerics/cartesian-poisson-3d-fem-fvm/README.md), [`numerics.global-matrix-free-action`](../../verify/numerics/global-matrix-free-action/README.md), [RFC 0075](../../rfcs/0075-fem-form-compiler-poisson-q1.md) |
| Cell-centered finite volume | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Orthogonal TPFA runs real or complex scalar diffusion and reaction in any dimension; 2D adds transient transport (first-order, periodic, minmod) and collocated Navier–Stokes with monolithic Newton. Not yet: unstructured meshes, wider flow boundaries, other pressure algorithms; [`fluid.cartesian-advection-diffusion-fvm-2d`](../../verify/fluid/cartesian-advection-diffusion-fvm-2d/README.md), [`fluid.cartesian-periodic-transport-fvm-2d`](../../verify/fluid/cartesian-periodic-transport-fvm-2d/README.md), [`fluid.cell-centered-navier-stokes-fvm-2d`](../../verify/fluid/cell-centered-navier-stokes-fvm-2d/README.md), [RFC 0072](../../rfcs/0072-collocated-incompressible-finite-volume.md) |
| Bounded higher-order FVM reconstruction | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | A Cartesian minmod reconstruction is a method choice for scalar transport along one axis (at least three cells, Courant number ≤ 1/2); the registered case shows order above 1.6, bounds, conservation and limiter activation. Not yet: multidirectional, unstructured or other limiters; [`fluid.cartesian-advection-diffusion-fvm-2d`](../../verify/fluid/cartesian-advection-diffusion-fvm-2d/README.md), [RFC 0069](../../rfcs/0069-conservative-cell-centered-transport.md) |
| Vertex-centered finite volume | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Control-volume construction and flux contracts remain |
| MPFA finite volume | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Non-orthogonal anisotropic diffusion remains |
| Discontinuous Galerkin | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Elliptic, hyperbolic, and conservation-law formulations remain |
| Finite difference | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Structured stencils and boundary closures remain |
| Spectral element | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | High-order bases, sum factorization, and quadrature remain |
| Boundary element | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Dense/hierarchical operators and singular quadrature remain |
| Particle methods | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | SPH/DEM/particle transport contracts remain |
| Meshfree methods | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Basis construction, support search, and stabilization remain |
| Conservative flux accounting | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Scalar FEM/FVM balances and transient transport keep interior fluxes equal and opposite, reconstruct boundary flux independently, and check mass balance per step and in total for donor-cell and limited MUSCL; periodic pairs are excluded from exterior flux; [`fluid.cartesian-advection-diffusion-fvm-2d`](../../verify/fluid/cartesian-advection-diffusion-fvm-2d/README.md), [`fluid.cartesian-periodic-transport-fvm-2d`](../../verify/fluid/cartesian-periodic-transport-fvm-2d/README.md) |
| Essential boundary conditions | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | Scalar Dirichlet paths |
| Bounded natural boundary conditions | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Outward flux boundary laws and packaged isotropic traction are verified, and zero-traction mixed boundaries run on Cartesian Q1 with recovered-traction convergence. Not yet: nonzero or general Neumann and Robin data, a broad boundary-law catalog; [`solid.packaged-elastic-boundary-2d`](../../verify/solid/packaged-elastic-boundary-2d/README.md), [`solid.mixed-boundary-elasticity-2d`](../../verify/solid/mixed-boundary-elasticity-2d/README.md) |
| Periodic boundary conditions | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | Geometric pairing, phase, and distributed constraints remain |
| Contact constraints | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | The finite nonspatial complementarity slice above does not provide geometric search, spatial contact assembly, friction, mortar, penalty, or augmented formulations. |
| Nonlinear material integration | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | History variables, consistent tangents, and local failure semantics remain |
| Stabilization methods | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | ⬜ | SUPG/PSPG, shock capturing, hourglass, and formulation-specific controls remain |
| A posteriori error estimation | 🟨 | 🟨 | ⬜ | ⬜ | Results give residual error indicators for steady scalar P1 diffusion on triangles with constant coefficient and zero boundary values, checked by hand-integrated and manufactured tests; a notebook combines marking, refinement and transfer. Not yet: certified constants, mixed boundaries, variable coefficients, other operators, goal-oriented estimates. |
| Solution transfer between meshes | ✅ | 🟨 | ⬜ | ⬜ | `State.interpolate(target_plan, source_result)` (Rust `interpolate_state`) moves continuous P1 nodal solutions onto a refined triangle mesh, keeping the Model and lifting boundary values; the next Run uses a newly resolved Plan. Not yet: conservative projection, history variables, coarsening, moving or distributed meshes. |
| Multi-domain coupling operators | ✅ | ✅ | ⬜ | ⬜ | One conforming two-Domain FSI case builds the shared interface from the mesh and couples the two P1 velocity traces, with interface forces cancelling row by row. Not yet: multiple or nonmatching interfaces, mortar, Nitsche or penalty coupling, partitioned coupling; [`fsi.fixed-reference-monolithic-step-2d`](../../verify/fsi/fixed-reference-monolithic-step-2d/README.md) |
