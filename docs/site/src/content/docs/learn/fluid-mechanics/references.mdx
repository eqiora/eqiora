## Physical derivations

- [David Tong, Fluid Mechanics](https://davidtong.org/teaching/fluid-mechanics/).
  Author-maintained undergraduate lecture notes. Use the continuum, stress and
  Navier–Stokes discussions alongside Chapters 1–4; the course also develops
  vorticity and topics beyond this introductory route.
- [MIT OpenCourseWare, Couette & Poiseuille Flows](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-25-advanced-fluid-mechanics-fall-2013/resources/mit2_25f13_couet_and_pois/),
  *2.25 Advanced Fluid Mechanics*, Fall 2013. Original course material for the
  wall-driven and pressure-driven experiments in Chapter 3.

## Numerical method and comparison problem

- D. N. Arnold, F. Brezzi and M. Fortin,
  [A stable finite element for the Stokes equations](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02576171),
  *Calcolo* **21**, 337–344 (1984). Original paper introducing the enriched
  velocity/linear-pressure element discussed in Chapter 5.
- [FeatFlow, DFG benchmark 2D-2: Reynolds-100 periodic cylinder flow](https://wwwold.mathematik.tu-dortmund.de/~featflow/en/benchmarks/cfdbenchmarking/flow/dfg_benchmark2_re100.html).
  The maintained specification defines geometry, inlet normalization, outlet,
  force observations, temporal measurement procedure and reference calculations.
  Read the setup before comparing a force or Strouhal value.

## Continue by the question you want to answer

- **How does heat spread or move with a fluid?** Start
  [heat transfer](/learn/heat-transfer/). Compare momentum diffusivity with thermal
  diffusivity before adding a transport analogy.
- **How does a solid resist the fluid load?** Start
  [structural mechanics](/learn/structural-mechanics/). The traction is shared;
  the constitutive law and primary unknown are different.
- **How should I judge a computed number?** Continue with
  [numerical simulation](/learn/numerical-simulation/) for errors, convergence
  and time integration.
- **Which assumptions belong in a model?** Use
  [mathematical modeling](/learn/mathematical-modeling/) as a companion reference,
  especially its balance, constitutive-law and boundary chapters.
