## Problem and accuracy

The workload uses the shipped
`Eqiora.Solid.LinearElasticity.IsotropicBalanceWithPotential2d` component through
ordinary compile → resolve → run. It is intrinsic two-dimensional isotropic
small-strain elasticity on the unit square in metres, with homogeneous displacement,
mu = 3 Pa, lambda = 2 Pa and the nonaffine potential declared in the
<ExactSourceLink path="tools/benchmarks/elasticity_fenicsx/README.md" kind="blob">frozen protocol</ExactSourceLink>.
This covers neither plane stress/strain nor dynamics, three dimensions or general materials.

Both tools use vector quadrilateral Q1, float64, two-point tensor Gauss assembly,
CG with identity preconditioning, zero initial guess, relative tolerance `1e-12`,
absolute tolerance `1e-14` and at most 10,000 iterations. The true algebraic
residual excludes essential rows in both tools. Eqiora stores the free system;
FEniCSx retains identity boundary rows. At N=32 there are 2,178 total and 1,922
free coefficients; the stored systems therefore differ in size.

Continuous displacement L2 and gradient H1 seminorm errors are integrated from
the exported Q1 fields against the independent manufactured analytic solution.
Ten-point quadrature is audited with twelve points. Boundary, finite-value,
true-residual and nonzero-load equilibrium checks passed. Cross-tool agreement
is not the oracle. L2 refinement orders were 1.9953, 1.9946, 1.9984; H1 orders
were 1.0004, 1.0003, 1.0000 in both tools.

L2 errors have units m²; gradient seminorm errors have units m.

## Ordinary execution

Each fresh child retains mesh/model/forms, but assembles and solves afresh for
each ordinary call. Field return is inside the clock; output inspection,
accuracy, protocol diagnostics, Result destruction and file writes are outside.
One cold call precedes separate profile and equilibrium probes, then two warmups
and seven samples. The probes may warm caches. These seven calls describe
within-process spread, not seven independent process repetitions.

## Mesh, preparation and memory

Mesh and preparation are single cold observations in milliseconds. Eqiora's
mesh clock includes GeometryGraph construction and mesh resolution/generation;
preparation covers compile and resolve. FEniCSx creates the mesh, then prepares
function space, boundary data and forms, including a fresh JIT cache per child.
Imports are separately recorded. Preparation wall time includes compiler-child
work; process CPU time excludes child CPU. Fresh processes and JIT directories
do not make filesystem or operating-system caches cold. These observations do
not establish preparation scaling or an end-to-end speed ranking.

Peak RSS is the Linux process high-water mark immediately after the cold field
return, before accuracy and profiling. It includes imports, mesh and preparation,
not just the operator. Child peak is the largest completed-child high-water
mark, not simultaneous aggregate process-tree memory. Do not add it to or
subtract it from the parent peak. Stage snapshots and final peaks are retained.

## Separate diagnostic phases

These are additional executions, not partitions of the ordinary medians.
Their native boundaries differ; no cross-tool phase speed ratio or subtraction
from ordinary timing is justified. All values below are milliseconds.

Eqiora assembly is its native scalar-region-finalization span; linear solve is
the reference-provider span. Solve self is exclusive time reported by the
profiler outside its child spans. FEniCSx assembly includes matrix/vector
allocation, assembly, lifting, ghost accumulation and essential boundary
application. Its linear bracket includes KSP allocation/configuration, zero
start, solve, independently recomputed residual, summary getters and KSP/residual
cleanup. Field recovery follows inside ordinary execution. Retained matrix/RHS
destruction and output inspection are outside it.

## Environment, provenance and reproduction

The campaign ran on an Intel Xeon Gold 6346 host with two sockets, 32 physical
cores and 64 logical CPUs. Worker CPU55 and sibling CPU23 were monitored;
supervisor CPU48 used a separate physical core. The bounded selection sample
observed 34.27% and 44.80% busy time on the worker and sibling, with host load
33.57/34.55/35.62. This was a busy shared host, with no reserved or isolated core.
Governors remained `powersave`; turbo remained enabled. Controls passed before
and after the campaign. OpenMP, OpenBLAS, MKL, BLIS and Rayon threads were one;
one rank and no GPU were used. Scratch and caches were home-backed.

Both interpreters were Python 3.13.18 with NumPy 2.5.4. FEniCSx used DOLFINx,
Basix and FFCx 0.11.0, UFL 2026.1.0, PETSc 3.25.6 and mpi4py 4.1.2 in the
locked isolated environment. Eqiora used the reference host-serial provider.
The wheel used Rust 1.89 release settings, fat LTO, stripping and existing
per-package size optimizations. Its installed package payload was verified
against the retained wheel before launch.

The
<ExactSourceLink path="docs/performance/2026-11-06-elasticity-fenicsx-runtime.json" kind="blob">source and artifact record</ExactSourceLink>
retains the actual wheel producer, wheel digest, separately frozen clean harness
and environment lock identity. These source identities are distinct from the
later publication commit.

The first launch was rejected before any worker because inherited supervisor
affinity excluded the selected worker. Correcting the outer launch affinity
produced the sole full campaign: eight workers and 56 samples. After its
successful terminal result, the outer audit encountered a Python module-name
collision. A fresh-process audit revalidated the original reports without
numerical work. Both failures remain recorded; no completed campaign or sample
was replaced to improve a ranking.

The
<ExactSourceLink path="docs/performance/2026-11-06-elasticity-fenicsx-runtime.json" kind="blob">measurement record</ExactSourceLink>
is an explicit public projection of original local evidence, not the original
report bytes. It normalizes filesystem prefixes, retains the original digests,
and leaves numerical observations unchanged. It includes every sample, candidate
field, phase inventory, memory snapshot, monitor observation, source/artifact
receipt, launch command and audit history. Original raw files remain retained
locally and bound by the inventory.

Reproduce the images from a clone with
<ExactSourceLink path="tools/benchmarks/elasticity_fenicsx/plot_runtime.py" kind="blob">plot_runtime.py</ExactSourceLink>
using NumPy and Matplotlib. It revalidates the committed fields before plotting.
To collect new observations, use the frozen protocol's installed-wheel driver
with `--mode benchmark`, the declared four levels, a new output directory and
an outer CPU/control monitor. No timing was rerun to write this page.

This result covers this manufactured Q1 workload only. It establishes no MPI,
GPU, three-dimensional elasticity or release-wide performance guarantee.
