Eqiora keeps a model's meaning, its numerical treatment, and its execution in separate layers.
Information moves in one direction:

```text
semantic meaning
      ↓
lowered contract
      ↓
realization
      ↓
adapter / execution provider
      ↓
artifact and evidence
```

Because nothing flows back up, a Python client, a numerical method, a storage format, or a
hardware adapter cannot become a second definition of what a model means.

## Model and realization

The semantic model holds the relations, their activation, typed ports, connections, and
identities. The realization holds the discretization and execution choices. Block diagrams,
state charts, physical networks, field views, Python, and Studio are all views or clients of
these two layers.

Artifacts record which model, realization, and execution produced a result; that record never
becomes part of the model. Verification manifests are kept separately and link specific claims
to reproducible evidence.
